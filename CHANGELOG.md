# What's new

## 1.1.2

- Every legendary amulet of your class, from the game's Codex (102 in all, one for each spell). Those that can drop now, because you picked their spell, come first and bright; the tooltip says which spell the rest wait for. An amulet a run has offered shows its real name and icon.
- "No spell effects" from the game's graphics options is in the quick menu.

## 1.1.1

**Quick menu and layouts**
- Click the gear on the minimap for modes, the layout and on/off for the panels you use most. Shift+click opens all settings, right click moves panels, Esc closes. The gear stays faint until the mouse comes near.
- Classic layout, as in the old WoW: your frame and the target's in the top left, buffs beside the minimap, the bar at the bottom centre. "Mine" brings your own layout back.
- The DPS meter can be switched off.

**Damage meter**
- New "hit/s" column (last 10 seconds) and "max" (biggest hit in the last minute), now filled in every damage-text mode.
- Pet hits are listed under the pet's name.
- Columns and the party meter fit their names and numbers.

**Panels**
- Folded panels show their most important line, with the numbers always in full.
- Right panels and pools move, scale and switch off together, or one by one.
- Damage taken: per second and the biggest hit. Guardian health as a number.
- Party loadouts under the party frames.

**Nameplates**
- Mobs, elites, guardians and bosses look different. Elite plates no longer flicker or shrink in crowds.
- Your own debuffs are larger above the bars, allies' smaller.
- Ally plates are narrower and taller; their height has its own setting. Their edge is in their class colour.
- The party's pets get a thin bar without a name instead of none (or none, with one switch).
- Enemy names have room and are no longer cut.
- Automatic raid marks; right click a portrait for its menu.

**Numbers**
- One short format everywhere (999k, 99.9m, 1.2b); full numbers when you want them.
- "Biggest hit" mode shows full numbers, crits in gold with "!".
- Units on totals, DPS rows and guardian health.
- Hover a skill for its DPS, share of damage and damage this run.

**Legendaries**
- The legendary row also learns legendaries worn by you and your allies (can be switched off).

## 1.1.0

**New panels** (each can be moved, locked, resized, folded and switched off)
- Bosses: a countdown to every boss of the run.
- Guardians: when each lair's guardian appears, then asleep or its health.
- Damage taken: what has hurt you this run.
- Run history: DPS now against your last and best run at the same moment.
- Jewelry pool: every ring, trinket and amulet, dimmed once the slots are full.
- Banished cards: click an icon in a pool to cross it out and list it.
- Bars for enemies beyond the game's own nameplates.
- Hero stats and a DPS graph against your last run, in the new detailed mode.

- Regeneration per second on the health bar, green healing numbers, and your hits with skill icons above the target.
- Full Russian interface, jewelry and skill names in all 14 game languages.
- Two-column character page with foldable groups; common and advanced settings.
- Legendary amulets of your class in the jewelry pool (confirmed ones).

**Warnings and map**
- A warning with a sound 10 seconds before each boss.
- Guardians and caches on the minimap, with pins at its edge for those further off.

**Modes and history**
- Default, detailed and hidden modes: `/nfui mode`, a button in Switches, or a key you bind.
- Hover a past run for its summary; `/nfui export` gives all runs as text.
- A colour-blind palette.

**Numbers**
- Bigger hits are larger and stay longer.
- Damage you take is one slow column above your health.
- Damage numbers no longer jump around or pile up in the middle of the screen.
- Current health only: percentages are gone from every bar.

**Look**
- Levels before names, coloured as in classic WoW.
- Enemy health drains smoothly and leaves a trail. Enemies next to you get a thin red edge that no longer hides their health.
- A new palette, after Catppuccin Mocha.
- The chat is rebuilt: clear narrow font, even margins, visible tabs, input above it.
- The piece of the old action bar at the bottom of the screen is gone.

**Settings**
- Switches tab: every on/off choice in one place. Blocks tab: sizes of the new panels and fonts.
- Right-click a panel in Movers to lock it.
- Reset now returns to this edit's layout, not the original addon's. Your run history is kept.
- `Check for updates` in the game, and `tools/update-nfUI.bat` to update in one step.

**Fixes**
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean settings no longer break the addon when the game's font is not available.
- Romanian letters on the bars.
- Upgrades keep what you moved: each change to your profile now runs only once.
